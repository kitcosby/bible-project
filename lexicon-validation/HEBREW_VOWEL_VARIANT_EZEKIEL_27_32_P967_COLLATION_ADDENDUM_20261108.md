# Ezekiel 27:32 — P967 collation clarification (2026-11-08)

## Result

A newly inspected scholarly collation clarifies the scope of P967's omission at Ezekiel 27:32. The omission is **not the entire verse and not the phrase “their sons.”** It is the closing question:

> מִי כְצֹר כְּדֻמָה בְּתוֹךְ הַיָּם  
> “Who is like Tyre, like one destroyed in the midst of the sea?”

Ingrid E. Lilly, *Papyrus 967: A Variant Literary Edition of Ezekiel* (Emory University dissertation, 2010), in the textual-variant list at printed p. 86 / PDF lines 3746–3754, records for Ezekiel 27:32:

- **(minus) 967 Z B Co Arab Tyc. Hi.test** — these witnesses omit the Hebrew closing question.
- **(ast. O)** followed by the Greek equivalent of “Who is like Tyre, like one destroyed in the midst of the sea” — the clause is marked as an asterisked reading in Origen's tradition.
- The Hebrew alignment is explicitly **מי כצור כדמה בתוך הים**.

This is a secondary scholarly collation of P967, not a direct inspection of the 1938 papyrus transcription or photograph. It nevertheless resolves an ambiguity in the shorthand “P967 omits v.32”: the omitted unit is specifically the closing question, not all of verse 32.

The report that P967 retains the preceding Greek phrase **οἱ υἱοὶ αὐτῶν** (“their sons”) is independently stated by Werner Bible Commentary. Taken together, these sources support the following carefully delimited description:

1. P967's text of Ezekiel 27:32 includes the “their sons will take up a lament” clause.
2. P967 omits the final “Who is like Tyre … in the midst of the sea?” question.
3. P967 also omits MT verse 31, yielding a shorter local sequence, but it does **not** omit the sons-reading in verse 32.

## Why this matters for the Hebrew variant

This evidence supports the existence of the “sons” reading in an early Greek textual form, but it does not prove that P967's Hebrew Vorlage had the exact pointed form בְּנֵיהֶם. Greek οἱ υἱοὶ αὐτῶν is an explicit rendering of “their sons”; the Greek witness is versional evidence for the lexical interpretation, not direct evidence for Hebrew vowel points.

Nor does the P967 omission of the closing question decide whether the MT's בְּנִיהֶם (“in their wailing”) is original, whether בְּנֵיהֶם (“their sons”) is original, or whether one reading arose as a gloss or contextual reinterpretation. The verse's literary form and the disputed word must be assessed as related but distinct textual questions.

## Evidential classification

- **Directly established by Lilly's published collation:** P967 is listed among witnesses omitting the final question in Ezekiel 27:32.
- **Reported by a separate commentary:** P967 retains “their sons” in the preceding clause.
- **Not directly established in this pass:** the exact papyrus line/column, physical condition, corrections, and apparatus of Johnson–Gehman–Kase 1938 at this locus.
- **Not established:** that the Hebrew Vorlage of P967 carried the exact Masoretic consonants with the alternative vocalization בְּנֵיהֶם, or that P967's reading is chronologically prior.

Keep adjudication **OPEN_TEXT_CRITICAL_REVIEW**. Preserve the WLC/OSHB source form בְּנִיהֶם, H5204, its morphology, all lexicon data, and the YLT text/alignment. Do not change group 504's orthographic cross-reference status.
