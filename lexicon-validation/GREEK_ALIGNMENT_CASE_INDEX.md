### Interpretation

Many cases are not actual contradictions. They reflect differences between word-level links and phrase-level or grammatical relationships. The strongest candidate legacy positional defects are Romans 1:27 and 2 Peter 2:9. 1 Timothy 3:2 is suspicious but requires exact production-sequence provenance verification.

No legacy or production rows were changed by this contextual review. Earlier shadow-only corrections to four alignment targets are documented in the separate overlap audit.
