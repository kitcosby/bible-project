

## 12. Verse-specific collation check in Lilly's dissertation (2026-11-08)

A direct search and inspection of Ingrid E. Lilly, *Papyrus 967: A Variant Literary Edition of Ezekiel* (Emory dissertation, 2010), now provides a more precise result for the P967 evidence at Ezekiel 27:32. The dissertation's variant list prints:

```
27:32
(minus) 967 Z B Co Arab Tyc.Hi.test]
(ast. O) τις ωσπερ τυρος κατασιγηθεισα εν μεσω
θαλασσης ≈ rel. = מי כצור כדמה בתוך הים MT
```

The entry identifies the omitted material as the **closing question** מי כצור כדמה בתוך הים (“Who is like Tyre, like one silenced/destroyed in the midst of the sea?”). It does not identify the preceding Greek phrase οἱ υἱοὶ αὐτῶν (“their sons”) as omitted. This confirms the precise scope of the minus in Lilly's collation and corrects any imprecise shorthand that might suggest P967 omits all of v.32.

Important limits:
- Lilly's entry is a published scholarly collation, not direct inspection of the Princeton papyrus plates in this session.
- The entry establishes the minus of the final question in P967 and the listed witnesses; it does not by itself independently transcribe the preceding “sons” clause.
- The claim that P967 retains “their sons” remains supported here by Werner's explicit report and by the extant Old Greek wording, but the Princeton transcription and papyrus image still need direct inspection before we label the papyrus word-level reading independently verified.
- Do not infer the Hebrew pointing of P967's Vorlage from the Greek phrase.

This distinction matters for textual criticism: the omission of the final question is a separate textual variant from the Hebrew vocalization dispute בְּנִיהֶם / בְּנֵיהֶם. The two should not be merged into one apparatus claim.

### Consequence for the witness matrix

| Proposition | Current status |
|---|---|
| P967 omits the closing question at 27:32 | Directly reported in Lilly's verse-specific collation |
