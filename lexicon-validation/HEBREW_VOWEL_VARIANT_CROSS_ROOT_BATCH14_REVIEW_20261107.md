# Hebrew vowel-only cross-root review — Batch 14

## Scope and method

Reviewed the next 15 pending Hebrew candidate groups using pointed forms, member/root assignments, Strong’s IDs, morphology, and extracted Hebrew verse contexts. This is member-level inspection, not exhaustive lexicographic adjudication. No semantic equivalence is approved and no lexical/root/morphology/alignment records are merged.

- Review date: 2026-11-07
- Groups inspected: 15
- Pending groups remaining: 159 (174 before this batch)
- Production/shadow database writes: none

## 1. `אחר֖י`

The common member אַחֲרֵי (H310) means “after”; the second member אַחֲרַי means “after me” (same preposition with 1cs suffix). The 37 occurrences include both forms; this is an inflection/suffix distinction within one lexical family.

## 2. `לפני֙`

לְפָנַי is “before me” (H6440 with 1cs suffix); לִפְנֵי is “before/in front of” (H6440, construct form). Same פנים lexical family with a different suffix/construct realization.

## 3. `ימ֣י`

יְמֵי is the construct plural “days of”; יָמַי is “my days” (both H3117). Same noun, construct versus 1cs suffix.

## 4. `וּבנ֖י`

וּבְנֵי is “and sons of” (H1121, construct plural); the other members include וּבְנָי / וּבָנַי, “and my sons” or “and my children” with 1cs suffix. The group spans three member keys but remains one noun family.

## 5. `פּנ֣י`

פְּנֵי is the construct form “face/surface of”; פָּנַי is “my face” (H6440 with 1cs suffix). Same noun family, construct versus suffixed form.

## 6. `את־בּנ֣י`

אֶת־בְּנִי is “my son” (H1121 with 1cs suffix); אֶת־בְּנֵי is “the sons” (H1121, construct plural). The direct-object marker את is retained in the signature; singular suffixed noun versus plural construct.

## 7. `מפּנ֥י`

מִפְּנֵי is “from/before the face of”; מִפָּנַי is “from before me” (H6440 with 1cs suffix). Same lexical family; suffix and pointing distinguish the forms.

## 8. `בּנ֤י`

בְּנִי is “my son”; בְּנֵי is “sons of”; בָּנַי is “my sons” (H1121). Three inflectional/construct forms of the same noun, not distinct roots.

## 9. `פּנ֥י`

פְּנֵי is “face/surface of”; פָּנַי is “my face” (H6440). Construct versus 1cs suffix.

## 10. `אלהי֙`

אֱלֹהֵי is “God/gods of” (construct plural); אֱלֹהַי is “my God” (H430 with 1cs suffix). Same אלהים noun family, construct versus suffixed form; context determines number/reference.

## 11. `אחרי֙`

אַחֲרֵי is “after”; אַחֲרַי is “after me” (H310 with 1cs suffix). Same preposition, unsuffixed versus suffixed form.

## 12. `אלה֖י`

אֱלֹהֵי is “God/gods of” (construct plural); אֱלֹהַי is “my God” (H430 with 1cs suffix). Same lexical family; construct versus suffixed form.

## 13. `מלּפנ֣י`

מִלִּפְנֵי is “from before/in front of”; מִלְּפָנַי is “from before me” (H6440 with 1cs suffix). Same פנים lexical family with different suffix realization.

## 14. `אבי֙`

אֲבִי is “father of” (construct form); אָבִי is “my father” (H1 with 1cs suffix). Same אב noun, construct versus suffixed form. One source morphology entry is tagged differently; retained as-is.

## 15. `בּני֙`

בְּנִי is “my son”; בְּנֵי is “sons of”; בָּנַי is “my sons” (H1121). Three member keys represent singular/plural and construct/suffixed forms of the same noun family.

## Disposition and limitations

Most cases in this batch are inflectional/construct-state or pronominal-suffix variants within the same lexeme family, rather than separate roots. They remain useful as orthographic cross-references but must not be interpreted as evidence of distinct lexical identity or merged as separate lexemes. Existing morphology and any unusual source tags are preserved without correction.
