# Hebrew vowel-only cross-root review — Batch 13

## Scope and method

Reviewed the next 15 pending Hebrew candidate groups using pointed forms, member/root assignments, Strong’s IDs, morphology, and extracted verse contexts. This is member-level inspection, not exhaustive lexicographic adjudication. No semantic equivalence is approved and no lexical/root/morphology/alignment records are merged.

- Review date: 2026-11-07
- Groups inspected: 15
- Pending groups remaining: 174 (189 before this batch)
- Production/shadow database writes: none

## 1. `מרע֑הוּ`

Job 39:8 מִרְעֵהוּ is “his pasture” (H4829); Ecclesiastes 4:4 מֵרֵעֵהוּ is “his companion/neighbor” (H7453). Pasture versus companion, with different prepositional morphology.

## 2. `מרע֥ה`

Isaiah 32:14 מִרְעֵה is “pasture” (H4829); Ecclesiastes 12:11 מֵרֹעֶה is “from a shepherd” (H7462; participial form). Noun versus verbal participle.

## 3. `ע֠לי`

1 Samuel 4:13 עֵלִי is the proper name Eli (H5941); Esther 4:16 עָלַי means “upon me” (H5921). Name versus preposition with suffix.

## 4. `עזּ֤י`

Exodus 15:2 עָזִּי is “my strength” (H5797); Nehemiah 11:22 עֻזִּי is the proper name Uzzi (H5813). Common noun with suffix versus name.

## 5. `עזּֽה׃`

Proverbs 21:14 עַזָּה is “fierce/strong” (H5794); Habakkuk 3:4 עֻזֹּה is “his strength” (H5797). Adjective versus noun with suffix.

## 6. `עליה`

Obadiah 1:1 עָלֶיהָ is “upon her” (H5921, preposition with 3fs suffix); 1 Chronicles 1:51 עליה is a proper-name entry (H5933). The exact name vocalization/gloss should be checked against the source lexicon; do not infer it from the unpointed token.

## 7. `עצבּ֣י`

Psalm 135:15 עֲצַבֵּי means “idols” (H6091); Isaiah 48:5 עָצְבִּי is “my graven image/idol” (H6090). Related but distinct lexical entries and number/suffix morphology.

## 8. `פּנ֑ינוּ`

Isaiah 53:6 פָּנִינוּ is “we turned/turned aside” (H6437, Qal perfect 1cp); Jeremiah 51:51 פָּנֵינוּ is “our face” (H6440, noun with 1cp suffix). Verb versus noun.

## 9. `שׁבים֙`

2 Chronicles 6:37 שִׁבְיָם means “their captivity” (H7633); 1 Samuel 7:3 שָׁבִים is a participial form from שוב, “returning” (H7725). Captivity noun versus returning participle.

## 10. `שׁמ֥י`

Isaiah 52:5 שְׁמִי is “my name” (H8034); Lamentations 3:66 שְׁמֵי is “heavens of” (H8064, plural construct). Singular noun with suffix versus plural construct.

## 11. `שׁמֽם׃`

Psalm 109:13 שְׁמָם is “their name” (H8034, noun with 3mp suffix); Lamentations 3:11 שֹׁמֵם is a verbal participle from שׁמם, “making/desolating” (H8076). Noun versus participle.

## 12. `ל֔ך`

The packet includes imperative לֵךְ “go!” (H3212) in 1 Samuel 9:3/17:37 and preposition לָךְ “to you” (2fs suffix) in numerous contexts (e.g. Genesis 4:6; 15:1). The latter member has no Strong’s value in the supplied records; preserve that null rather than filling it by inference. This is a high-frequency orthographic collision, not a semantic link.

## 13. `מפּנ֖י`

Most occurrences are מִפְּנֵי “from/before the face of” (H6440); Jeremiah 4:1 has מִפָּנַי “from before me” with 1cs suffix. Same lexeme family but distinct suffix realization; retain as an orthographic variant only.

## 14. `ל֑ך`

Exodus 4:12/Jeremiah 40:5 לֵךְ is “go!” (H3212); the other member לָךְ is “to you” (2fs suffix; no Strong’s value in the supplied records). Same caution as batch 13’s ל֔ך group: no semantic equivalence.

## 15. `על־פּנ֣י`

The dominant form עַל־פְּנֵי (H6440) occurs in phrases such as Genesis 1:2 and Genesis 1:29; Job 4:15 has עַל־פָּנַי, “upon my face/before me,” with a 1cs suffix. Both belong to the פנים lexical family; this is a form/suffix distinction, not evidence for a different lexeme.

## Disposition and limitations

All 15 rows are marked `reviewed_member_level` to record inspection only. The two לָךְ groups include a member without a Strong’s value in the source records; this has been preserved, not guessed. The proper-name entry עליה requires source-lexicon verification. The מפּני and על־פּני cases include suffix/form differences within the same lexical family. All remain orthographic candidates only.
