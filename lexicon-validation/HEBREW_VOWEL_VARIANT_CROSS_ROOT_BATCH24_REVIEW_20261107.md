## 1. `ועבד֖י`

וְעַבְדֵי is “and servants of” (H5650, plural construct); וַעֲבָדַי is “and my servants” (plural with 1cs suffix).

## 2. `ועבדתּ֑ם`

וַעֲבַדְתֶּם is “and you (plural) served/will serve” (H5647, 2mp); וַעֲבַדְתָּם is “and you (singular) served/will serve them” (2ms with 3mp object suffix). Exact tense/aspect rendering is contextual.

## 3. `ועמּ֣י`

וְעַמִּי is “and my people” (H5971, singular with 1cs suffix); וְעַמֵּי is “and peoples of” (plural construct).

## 4. `ושׂמתּ֖ם`

## 6. `כּנפי֙`

## 7. `ל֝פנ֗י`

לְפָנַי is “before me” (H6440 with 1cs suffix); לִפְנֵי is “before/in front of” (construct form).

## 8. `ל֨ך`

לֵךְ is “go!” (H3212, Qal imperative 2ms); לָךְ is “to you” (preposition ל + 2fs suffix, Strong’s absent). Unrelated grammatical units.

## 9. `לאב֣י`

## 10. `לאדנ֣י`

לַאֲדֹנֵי is “to the lords/masters of” (H113, plural construct); לַאדֹנִי is “to my lord/master” (singular with 1cs suffix).

## 11. `לעמּ֣י`

לְעַמִּי is “to/for my people” (H5971, singular with 1cs suffix); לְעַמֵּי is “to/for peoples of” (plural construct).

## 12. `לפני֮`

Same basic contrast as the earlier לפני group: לְפָנַי “before me” versus לִפְנֵי “before/in front of.” This is a distinct accent-bearing orthographic signature, not an additional lexical distinction.

## 13. `מלּפני֙`

מִלְּפָנַי is “from before me” (H6440 with 1cs suffix); מִלִּפְנֵי is “from before/in front of” (construct form).

## 14. `ממּע֖י`

מִמְּעֵי is “from the innards of” (H4578, plural construct); מִמֵּעַי is “from my innards” (plural with 1cs suffix).

## 15. `מפּנ֗י`

מִפְּנֵי is “from before/on account of” (H6440, construct); מִפָּנַי is “from before me” (same noun/preposition with 1cs suffix).

## Notes

Most candidates are inflectional contrasts involving construct forms versus 1cs suffixes, or person/number and object suffixes on verbs. The לֵךְ / לָךְ collision is unrelated imperative versus preposition-plus-suffix. Several פני groups recur with distinct cantillation signatures; they are separate inventory groups but repeat the same grammatical contrast. No lexical or database records were changed.
