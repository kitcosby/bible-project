## 1. `אֽיבי֙`

אֹיְבֵי is “enemies of” (H341, participial/nominal construct plural); אֹיְבַי is “my enemies” (plural with 1cs suffix). Same אויב lexeme family.

## 2. `אב֨י`

אֲבִי is “father of” (H1, construct); אָבִי is “my father” (same noun with 1cs suffix).

## 3. `אח֧י`

אֲחֵי is “brothers of” (H251, plural construct); אַחַי is “my brothers” (plural with 1cs suffix).

## 4. `אל־אח֖י`

אֶל־אֲחֵי is “to/toward the brothers of”; אַל־אַחַי is “not/do not … my brothers” depending clause (אַל as prohibitive/negative particle plus “my brothers”). The preposition אֶל and particle אַל are distinct; the shared consonants do not make these equivalent.

## 13. `הא֥ירה`

הֵאִירָה is Qal/Hiphil? source morphology marks Hiphil perfect 3fs (“she gave light/illuminated,” H215); הָאִירָה is Hiphil imperative 2ms with directive ה (“make shine/give light!”). Same אור root, finite 3fs versus imperative 2ms; verify the source stem code against the project morphology legend before translating.

## 14. `האכ֥ל`

הֵאָכֹל is parsed as an infinitive form of אכל (H398; likely passive/Niphal infinitive construct in this vocalization); הָאֹכֵל is article + Qal participle masculine singular (“the one eating”). Same root, infinitive versus participial noun phrase. Flag the exact stem/value of HVNa for morphology-legend verification.

## 15. `הב֥א`

הֲבָא is interrogative ה + Qal perfect 3ms (“has he come?”; H935); הָבֵא is Hiphil imperative 2ms (“bring!”). Same בוא root family, interrogative finite form versus causative imperative.
