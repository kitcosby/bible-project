## Hypotheses withdrawn or held

### Withdrawn
- Acts 26:18: the earlier B proposal confused the disputed τοῦ/καί before ἐπιστρέψαι with the later conjunction before “the authority.” YLT “to turn” supports τοῦ, branch A. Do not switch to B.
- 2 John 1:5: English “not as writing” does not prove a Greek participle. The current finite γράφω (A) is syntactically natural. Do not switch to B.

### Held unresolved
- Acts 15:32: τε versus δέ cannot be uniquely resolved from “Judas also and Silas.” Printed editions differ; preserve both.
- Acts 19:27: δέ versus τε is not uniquely recoverable from the YLT English connective phrase; preserve the current candidate pending review.
- Acts 21:8: YLT “we came” supports ἤλθομεν, but οἱ περὶ τὸν Παῦλον appears in differing witness combinations. Retain a witness-bundle caveat; do not synthesize unsupported combinations.

## Key findings and evidence notes

- 1 Corinthians 15:31: YLT “glorying of you” aligns with ὑμετέραν. Modern critical and Byzantine editions commonly print ὑμετέραν; ἡμετέραν is a received-text/minority alternative in some printed editions. See https://lemma.gebible.app/v/1-corinthians/15/31 and https://bibelvers.no/en/youngs-literal-translation/1-corinthians/15/31.
- 2 John 1:3: YLT “with you” aligns with ὑμῶν. Scrivener 1894 prints ὑμῶν, while Nestle/WH/SBL print ἡμῶν; manuscript support is mixed. See https://biblehub.com/text/2_john/1-3.htm and https://greeknewtestament.net/2jn1-3.
- 2 Corinthians 11:10: YLT “shall not be stopped” aligns with φραγήσεται. A critical commentary notes the alternative σφραγίζεται in the TR may be conjectural. See https://lemma.gebible.app/v/2-corinthians/11/10.
- Acts 21:8: YLT has “we came”; the variant ἤλθομεν is supported by NA/WH and other editions, while TR/Byzantine readings vary with the additional phrase about Paul’s companions. See https://lemma.gebible.app/v/acts/21/8 and https://openbible.com/text/acts/21-8.htm.
- Acts 26:20: YLT “I was preaching” aligns with ἀπήγγελλον (first-person imperfect); TR/Byzantine tradition has ἀπαγγέλλων. See https://www.studylight.org/interlinear-study-bible/greek/acts-26-20.html and https://greeknewtestament.net/ac26-20.
- Luke 8:31: YLT singular “he was calling” aligns with παρεκάλει. Scrivener 1894 has the plural, while Elzevir/Beza/Stephanus and Byzantine editions have the singular. See https://www.textusreceptusbibles.com/Interlinear/42008031.
- Revelation 11:2: YLT “without the sanctuary” aligns with ἔξωθεν (“outside”), not ἔσωθεν (“within”). Critical/Byzantine editions favor ἔξωθεν; Scrivener TR has ἔσωθεν. See https://lemma.gebible.app/v/revelation/11/2.
- Revelation 16:5: YLT “who shalt be” aligns with ὁ ἐσόμενος, a received-text/Beza reading; major critical/Byzantine editions read ὁ ὅσιος. Cambridge commentary regards the latter as better supported. See https://bibelvers.no/en/youngs-literal-translation/revelation/16/5 and https://lemma.gebible.app/v/revelation/16/5.
