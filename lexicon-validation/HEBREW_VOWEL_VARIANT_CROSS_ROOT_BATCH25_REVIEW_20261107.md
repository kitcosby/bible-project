## 1. `משׁחית֖ם`

מַשְׁחִיתִם is a masculine plural participle in construct (“destroyers of”); מַשְׁחִיתָם is a masculine singular participle with 3mp object suffix (“destroying them”). Both derive from שחת (H7843); number/construction versus object suffix differ.

## 2. `עבד֖י`

עֲבָדַי is “my servants” (H5650, plural with 1cs suffix); עַבְדֵי is “servants of” (plural construct).

## 3. `עזבתּ֖ם`

עֲזַבְתֶּם is “you (plural) left/abandoned” (H5800, 2mp perfect); עֲזַבְתָּם is “you (singular) left/abandoned them” (2ms with 3mp object suffix).

## 4. `ער֖י`

עָרֵי is “cities of” (H5892, feminine plural construct); עָרַי is “my cities” (feminine plural with 1cs suffix).

## 5. `פּנ֛י`

פְּנֵי is “face/surface of” (H6440, construct); פָּנַי is “my face” (same noun with 1cs suffix).

## 6. `צוּר֥י`

צוּרִי is “my rock” (H6697, singular with 1cs suffix); צוּרֵי is “rocks of” (plural construct).

## 7. `רדפ֣י`

רֹדְפֵי is “pursuers of” (H7291, participial plural construct); רֹדְפַי is “my pursuers” (same participle with 1cs suffix).

## 8. `שׂנא֥י`

שֹׂנְאֵי is “those hating/enemies of” (H8130, participial plural construct); שֹׂנְאַי is “those who hate me/my haters” (same participle with 1cs suffix).

## 9. `על֖יה`

עֳלֶיהָ (Isaiah 37:33) and עָלֶיהָ (the other 32 occurrences in the extracted contexts) are both parsed as the preposition עַל with a 3fs suffix (“upon her/it”), and share the same source Strong’s H5921 and morphology HR/Sp3fs. The vowel difference appears to be a vocalization/lexical-member assignment distinction rather than a clear grammatical or semantic contrast. Preserve the two source member/root assignments; flag for Masoretic/source-data review rather than merge.
