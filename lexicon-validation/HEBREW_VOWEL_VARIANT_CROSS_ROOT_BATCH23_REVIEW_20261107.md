## 1. `העמ֣ד`

הַעֲמֵד is a Hiphil imperative 2ms of עמד (“stand/set [it] up,” H5975); הָעֹמֵד is article + Qal participle masculine singular (“the one standing”). Same root, imperative versus participial form.

## 2. `הק֣לּוּ`

הֲקַלּוּ in Genesis 8:8 is interrogative ה + Qal perfect 3cp (“have [the waters] diminished/abated?”); הֵקַלּוּ in Ezekiel 22:7 is Hiphil perfect 3cp (“they treated lightly/dishonored”). Same קלל root family, different stem and clause function.

## 3. `ו֝ידע֗י`

וְיֹדְעֵי is “and those knowing” (H3045, participle plural construct); וְיֹדְעַי is “and those who know me” (same participle with 1cs suffix).

## 4. `וּבכ֥ה`

וּבְכָה is “and in you” (preposition ב + 2ms suffix; Strong’s absent); וּבָכֹה is “and weeping/to weep” (H1058, infinitive construct). Distinct grammatical units.

## 5. `וֽירשׁתּ֕ם`

וִירִשְׁתֶּם is “and you (plural) shall possess/inherit” (H3423, 2mp); וִירִשְׁתָּם is “and you (singular) shall possess them” (2ms with 3mp object suffix). Same verb, person/number and object marking differ.

## 6. `וא֣דע֔ה`

וְאֵדְעָה is 1cs cohortative (“and let me know / so that I may know,” H3045); וָאֵדְעָה is 1cs wayyiqtol (“and I knew/came to know”). Same verb; cohortative versus narrative finite form.

## 7. `ואדנ֖י`

וַאֲדֹנֵי is “and lords/masters of” (H136, plural construct); וַאֲדֹנָי is “and my Lord” (same noun with 1cs suffix). Context determines referent/title.

## 8. `ואח֣י`

וְאַחַי is “and my brothers” (H251, plural with 1cs suffix); וַאֲחִי is “and brother of” (singular construct).

## 9. `ואיב֣י`

וְאֹיְבֵי is “and enemies of” (H341, participial/nominal construct plural); וְאֹיְבַי is “and my enemies” (plural with 1cs suffix).

## 10. `ואמר֤ה`

וְאָמְרָה is “and she said” (H559, 3fs); וָאֹמְרָה is “and I said” (1cs wayyiqtol). Person/subject differs.

## 11. `ואשׁ֥יבה`

וְאָשִׁיבָה is a 1cs Hiphil cohortative (“and let me bring back/restore,” H7725); וָאָשִׁיבָה is a 1cs Hiphil wayyiqtol (“and I brought back/restored”). Same verb and stem; cohortative versus narrative form.

## 12. `ואת־בּני֙`

וְאֶת־בְּנִי is “and my son” (H1121, singular with 1cs suffix); וְאֶת־בְּנֵי is “and sons of” (plural construct). The direct-object marker and maqaf are retained in the signature.

## 13. `ויּרא֣ה`

וַיִּרְאֶה is “and he saw” (H7200, 3ms); וַיִּרְאֶהָ is “and he saw her/it” (same verb with 3fs object suffix). The pointed forms differ by suffix marking in the full word.

## 14. `וימ֣י`

וְיָמַי is “and my days” (H3117, plural with 1cs suffix); וִימֵי is “and days of” (plural construct).

## 15. `ול֖ך`

וְלֵךְ is “and go!” (H3212, imperative 2ms); וָלָךְ is “and to you” (preposition ל + 2fs suffix, Strong’s absent). Distinct grammatical units.

## Notes

This batch includes inflectional differences (construct/suffix, person/number, cohortative/wayyiqtol) and clear unrelated grammatical collisions: וּבְכָה (“in you”) versus וּבָכֹה (“weeping”), and וְלֵךְ (“and go”) versus וָלָךְ (“and to you”). The הֲקַלּוּ / הֵקַלּוּ contrast is same-root but different verbal stem and meaning. No lexical or database records were changed.
