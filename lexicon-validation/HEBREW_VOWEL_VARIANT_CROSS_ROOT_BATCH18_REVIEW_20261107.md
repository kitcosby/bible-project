This is member-level inspection, not exhaustive lexicographic adjudication. No semantic equivalence is approved and no lexical/root/morphology/alignment records are merged.

## 1. `אלה֛י`

אֱלֹהֵי is “God/gods of” (H430, construct plural); אֱלֹהַי is “my God” (same noun with 1cs suffix). The latter is morphologically tagged as a suffixed form in the source. Context determines reference; the orthographic group does not settle number or theology.

## 2. `את־כּל־דּבר֖י`

אֶת־כָּל־דְּבָרַי is “all my words” (H1697, plural with 1cs suffix); אֶת־כָּל־דִּבְרֵי is “all the words of” (plural construct). The token includes direct-object marker and maqaf-linked כָּל; all are retained by the signature.

## 3. `את־כּל־דּברי֙`

אֶת־כָּל־דְּבָרַי is “all my words”; אֶת־כָּל־דִּבְרֵי is “all the words of” (H1697). Same דבר noun; pronominal suffix versus construct.

## 4. `דּבר֨י`

דְּבָרַי is “my words” (H1697, plural with 1cs suffix); דִּבְרֵי is “words of” (plural construct). Same דבר noun, differing suffix/construct morphology.

## 5. `דּרכ֣י`

דְּרָכַי is “my ways” (H1870, plural with 1cs suffix); דַּרְכֵי is “ways of” (plural construct). Same דרך noun.

## 6. `וא֣מר֔ה`

וְאָמְרָה is “and she said” (H559, 3fs form); וָאֹמְרָה is “and I said” (1cs wayyiqtol with paragogic ה in the source morphology). Person/conjugation differs within אמר.

## 7. `ואע֣ידה`

וְאָעִידָה is “and I will testify/call to witness” (H5749, 1cs cohortative); וָאָעִידָה is “and I testified/called to witness” (1cs wayyiqtol). Same verb; cohortative versus narrative conjugation.

## 8. `ול֣ך`

וְלֵךְ is “and go!” (H3212, imperative); וְלָךְ is “and to you” (2fs pronominal suffix, no Strong’s value in the source member). Different lexemes; preserve the missing Strong’s value.

## 9. `ל֗ך`

לֵךְ is “go!” (H3212, imperative); לָךְ is “to you” (2fs suffix, Strong’s null in source). Different verb/preposition analyses; not semantic variants.

## 10. `משׁפּט֣י`

מִשְׁפְּטֵי is “judgments/ordinances of” (H4941, plural construct); מִשְׁפָּטִי is “my judgment” (singular with 1cs suffix); מִשְׁפָּטַי is “my judgments” (plural with 1cs suffix). Same משפט noun with number and suffix/construct variation.

## 11. `משׁפּט֥י`

מִשְׁפְּטֵי is “judgments of” (H4941, plural construct); מִשְׁפָּטִי is “my judgment” (singular with 1cs suffix). Same משפט noun.

## 12. `על־דּבר֖י`

עַל־דְּבָרִי is “concerning my word” (H1697, singular with 1cs suffix); עַל־דִּבְרֵי is “concerning the words of” (plural construct). Preposition and maqaf are preserved; noun number/suffix differs.

## 13. `ראשׁ֖י`

רָאשֵׁי is “heads/chiefs of” (H7218, plural construct); רֹאשִׁי is “my head” (singular with 1cs suffix). Same ראש noun family.

## 14. `שׁמעתּ֑ם`

שְׁמַעְתֶּם is “you (mp) heard” (H8085, Qal perfect 2mp); שְׁמַעְתָּם is “you (ms) heard them” (same verb with 3mp object suffix). Person and object suffix differ.

## 15. `את־עבד֤י`

אֶת־עֲבָדַי is “my servants” (H5650, plural with 1cs suffix); אֶת־עַבְדֵי is “the servants of” (plural construct). Direct-object marker and maqaf retained; same עבד noun.

## Disposition and limitations

Most groups are inflectional forms within the same lexical families: construct versus suffix, singular versus plural, or verbal conjugation/person differences. The לֵךְ / לָךְ group is a genuine unrelated-lexeme collision; the prepositional member lacks a Strong’s value in the source and that null is retained. Existing lexical/morphological data are not changed. These rows record member-level inspection only.
