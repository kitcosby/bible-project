# Hebrew vowel-only cross-root review — Batch 12

## Scope and method

Reviewed the next 15 pending Hebrew candidate groups using the extracted pointed forms, member/root assignments, Strong’s identifiers, morphology, and Hebrew verse contexts. This is member-level inspection of source-database evidence, not exhaustive lexicographic adjudication. The groups remain orthographic cross-reference candidates only; no semantic equivalence is approved and no lexical, root, morphology, or alignment records are merged.

## 1. `יראתם֙`

Deuteronomy 5:5 יְרֵאתֶם֙ is “you feared” (H3372; Qal perfect 2mp); Isaiah 29:13 יִרְאָתָם֙ is “their fear” (H3373; nominal form with 3mp suffix in the database morphology). Verbal versus nominal morphology.

## 2. `ירע֣ם`

Hosea 4:16 יִרְעֵם is “he will shepherd/feed them” (H7462); Psalm 98:7 יִרְעַם is “the sea roars” (H7481). Different roots and clause subjects.

## 3. `כּלּֽה׃`

Jeremiah 16:9 כַּלָּה is “bride” (H3618); 2 Samuel 2:9 כֻּלֹּה means “all of it / the whole of it” (H3605). Distinct nominal lexemes.

## 4. `לבנ֔ות`

Genesis 30:37 לְבָנוֹת is “white” (H3836), describing the peeled streaks; Ezra 1:5 לִבְנוֹת is the infinitive “to build” (H1129). Adjective versus infinitive.

## 5. `לבנ֔ים`

Genesis 11:3 לְבֵנִים means “bricks” (H3843); Isaiah 38:19 לְבָנִים is “sons” (H1121). Distinct noun lexemes.

## 6. `להרפ֖ה`

Jeremiah 19:11 לְהֵרָפֵה is an infinitive from רפא, “to be healed” (H7495); 2 Samuel 21:22 לְהָרָפָה refers to Rapha (H7497), the proper name.

## 7. `להרפֽא׃`

Deuteronomy 28:27 לְהֵרָפֵא is “to be healed” (H7495); 1 Chronicles 20:6 לְהָרָפָא is “to Rapha” (H7497), a proper name. Parallel verb-versus-name distinction.

## 8. `מ֝עשׂ֗הוּ`

Job 4:17 מֵעֹשֵׂהוּ means “from his Maker” (H6213; participial form with suffix); Proverbs 16:11 מַעֲשֵׂהוּ means “his work/deed” (H4639). Agent/participle versus product noun.

## 9. `מֽעבר֙`

Numbers 32:7 מֵעֲבֹר is “from crossing/passing” (H5674; infinitive); Isaiah 30:32 מַעֲבַר is “a passage/place of crossing” (H4569). Verbal infinitive versus noun.

## 10. `מה֖ר`

Psalm 3:5 מֵהַר is “from [his] holy mountain” (H2022); 2 Chronicles 18:8 מַהֵר is the imperative “hasten!” (H4116). Prepositional noun phrase versus imperative.

## 11. `מח֣ר`

Numbers 33:33 מֵחֹר is “from Hor” (H2735), a place-name; 1 Samuel 20:18 מָחָר is “tomorrow” (H4279). Proper place-name versus temporal expression.

## 12. `מלכּ֔ם`

1 Kings 11:5 מִלְכֹּם is Milcom, the Ammonite deity (H4445); Jeremiah 30:9 מַלְכָּם is “their king” (H4428). Proper name versus common noun with 3mp suffix.

## 13. `מעונֽך׃`

Psalm 91:9 מְעוֹנֶךָ is “your dwelling” (H4583); Job 11:6 מֵעֲוֹנֶךָ is “from your iniquity” (H5771). Distinct lexical and morphological analyses despite the shared consonantal signature.

## 14. `מרא֥ות`

Isaiah 33:15 מֵרְאוֹת is “from seeing” (H7200; infinitive); Ezekiel 1:1 מַרְאוֹת is “visions” (H4759; plural noun). Infinitive versus nominal plural.

## 15. `מרב֙`

1 Samuel 18:17 מֵרַב is Merab, Saul’s daughter (H4764); Nahum 3:4 מֵרֹב means “from the abundance/multitude” (H7230). Proper name versus common noun.

## Disposition and limitations

All 15 rows are marked `reviewed_member_level` to record inspection, not approval of shared meaning. Strong’s IDs and morphology are treated as existing database evidence and are not corrected here. The “your dwelling” / “from your iniquity” pair is a clear warning that consonant-only signatures can group unrelated lexemes; that is expected under the requested orthographic rule and must not be mistaken for semantic equivalence. No production or shadow database writes were performed.
