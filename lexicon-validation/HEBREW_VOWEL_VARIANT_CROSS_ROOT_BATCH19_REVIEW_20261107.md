# Hebrew vowel-only cross-root review — Batch 19

## Scope and method

Reviewed the next 15 pending Hebrew candidate groups using pointed forms, member/root assignments, Strong’s identifiers, morphology, and extracted verse contexts. This is member-level inspection, not exhaustive lexicographic adjudication. No semantic equivalence is approved and no lexical/root/morphology/alignment records are merged.

## 1. `בּיד֖י`

בְּיָדִי is “in/by my hand” (H3027, singular with 1cs suffix); בִּידֵי is “in/by the hands of” (plural construct). Same יד noun; number and suffix/construct differ.

## 2. `הרא֥ה`

הֲרֹאֶה in Ezekiel 8:6 is a question/interrogative plus participial form (“is [he] seeing?” / “do you see?” depending syntax); הֶרְאָה in Genesis 41:28, 48:11, Exodus 27:8 is Hiphil perfect 3ms “he showed.” Same ראה root, but distinct verbal stem/form and syntax. Verify exact clause-level subject in context before translating the Ezekiel phrase.

## 3. `ואב֣י`

וְאָבִי is “and my father” (H1, noun with 1cs suffix); וַאֲבִי is “and the father of” (construct). Same אב noun; suffix versus construct.

## 4. `ועינ֖י`

וְעֵינִי is “and my eye” (H5869, singular with 1cs suffix); וְעֵינֵי is “and eyes of” (plural construct).

## 5. `ושׂר֤י`

וְשָׂרֵי is “and princes/chiefs of” (H8269, plural construct); וְשָׂרַי is “and my princes/chiefs” (plural with 1cs suffix). Same שר noun.

## 6. `יד֗י`

יְדֵי is “hands of” (H3027, plural construct); יָדִי is “my hand” (singular with 1cs suffix); יָדַי is “my hands” (plural with 1cs suffix). Three inflectional patterns of יד.

## 7. `לאלה֣י`

לֵאלֹהֵי is “to/for God(s) of” (H430, construct plural); לֵאלֹהַי is “to/for my God” (same noun with 1cs suffix).

## 8. `לב֥א`

לְבֹא in Numbers 13:21 is a place-name (Lebô) according to the source’s proper-name morphology; לָבֹא in Exodus 12:23, Deuteronomy 20:19, 30:18 is the infinitive “to come/enter” (H935). This is a proper-name versus verb collision, not semantic equivalence; geographic identification of the place-name should be checked against a lexicon/atlas.

## 9. `לע֣יר`

לְעִיר is “to a city” (H5892, noun); לָעִיר in 2 Kings 19:13 and Isaiah 37:13 is classified by the source as a place-name, whereas Micah 6:9 has a prefixed/article noun form. This group may combine a proper-name parse with ordinary city noun; verify the place-name assignment and syntax before any lexical conclusion.

## 10. `מעל֖י`

מֵעָלַי is “from upon me/off me” (H5921 with 1cs suffix); מֵעֲלֵי is “from upon/from over” in construct/prepositional form. Same על preposition, suffix versus construct.

## 11. `על־דּברי֙`

עַל־דְּבָרַי is “concerning my words” (H1697, plural with 1cs suffix); עַל־דִּבְרֵי is “concerning the words of” (plural construct).

## 12. `צר֥י`

צָרֵי is “enemies/adversaries of” (H6862, plural construct); צָרַי is “my enemies/adversaries” (plural with 1cs suffix). Same צר noun.

## 13. `אב֤י`

אֲבִי is “father of” (H1, construct); אָבִי is “my father” (same noun with 1cs suffix).

## 14. `את־עבד֣י`

אֶת־עֲבָדַי is “my servants” (H5650, plural with 1cs suffix); אֶת־עַבְדֵי is “the servants of” (plural construct). Direct-object marker and maqaf retained.

## 15. `בּא֥י`

בָּאֵי is a participial construct plural (“those entering/coming into,” H935); בֹּאִי is the infinitive “my coming/entering” or “my entrance” (H935 with 1cs suffix, source morphology HVqc/Sp1cs). Same בוא verb family; participle versus infinitive with suffix.

## Disposition and limitations

Most cases are inflectional variants within the same lexeme family. Two cases merit additional lexical/geographic verification: the alleged place-name in Numbers 13:21 (`לְבֹא`) versus the infinitive of בוא, and the place-name parse in 2 Kings 19:13 / Isaiah 37:13 (`לָעִיר`) versus ordinary “to the city” morphology elsewhere. Ezekiel 8:6's interrogative/participle analysis under הרא֥ה also requires clause-level syntax review. These are flags, not corrections to source data.
