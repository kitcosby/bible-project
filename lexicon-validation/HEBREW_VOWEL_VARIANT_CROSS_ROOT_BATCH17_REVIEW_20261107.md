# Hebrew vowel-only cross-root review — Batch 17

## Scope and method

Reviewed the next 15 pending Hebrew candidate groups using pointed forms, member/root assignments, Strong’s identifiers, morphology, and extracted verse contexts. This is member-level inspection, not exhaustive lexicographic adjudication. No semantic equivalence is approved and no lexical/root/morphology/alignment records are merged.

## 1. `לפנ֛י`

לְפָנַי is “before me” (H6440 with 1cs suffix); לִפְנֵי is “before/in front of” (construct form). Same פנים noun family, construct versus suffixed form.

## 2. `עבד֛י`

עֲבָדַי is “my servants” (H5650, plural with 1cs suffix); עַבְדֵי is “servants of” (plural construct). Same עבד noun, suffix versus construct.

## 3. `עמּ֥י`

עַמִּי is “my people” (H5971, singular with 1cs suffix); עַמֵּי is “peoples of” (plural construct). Same עם noun family; number and suffix/construct differ.

## 4. `ראשׁי֙`

רָאשֵׁי is “heads/chiefs of” (H7218, plural construct); רֹאשִׁי is “my head” (singular with 1cs suffix). Same ראש noun.

## 5. `איב֥י`

אֹיְבִי is “my enemy” (H341, singular with 1cs suffix); אֹיְבֵי is “enemies of” (plural construct); אֹיְבַי is “my enemies” (plural with 1cs suffix). Three inflectional forms of אויב.

## 6. `את־דּברי֙`

אֶת־דְּבָרִי is “my word” (H1697, singular with 1cs suffix); אֶת־דְּבָרַי is “my words” (plural with 1cs suffix); אֶת־דִּבְרֵי is “words of” (plural construct). Number and suffix/construct morphology vary.

## 7. `את־פּני֙`

אֶת־פְּנֵי is “the face/surface of” (H6440, construct); אֶת־פָּנַי is “my face” (same noun with 1cs suffix). Direct-object marker and maqaf are retained.

## 8. `ואתּנ֤ה`

וְאֶתְּנָה is “and I will give” (H5414, cohortative); וָאֶתְּנָה is “and I gave” (1cs wayyiqtol). Same נתן verb; conjugation/discourse morphology differs.

## 9. `ואתּנ֥ה`

וְאֶתְּנָה is “and I will give” (H5414, cohortative); וָאֶתְּנָה is “and I gave” (1cs wayyiqtol). Same נתן verb, different verbal conjugations.

## 10. `לעיני֙`

לְעֵינֵי is “to/before the eyes of” (H5869, construct); לְעֵינַי is “to/before my eyes” (same noun with 1cs suffix).

## 11. `לפנ֗י`

לְפָנַי is “before me” (H6440 with 1cs suffix); לִפְנֵי is “before/in front of” (construct form). Same פנים noun family.

## 12. `מעשׂ֣י`

מַעֲשֵׂי is “works/deeds of” (H4639, plural construct); מַעֲשַׂי is “my works/deeds” (same noun with 1cs suffix).

## 13. `עינ֥י`

עֵינִי is “my eye” (H5869, singular with 1cs suffix); עֵינֵי is “eyes of” (plural construct); עֵינַי is “my eyes” (plural with 1cs suffix). The three members are number/suffix/construct variants of עין.

## 14. `עשׂיתֽם׃`

עֲשִׂיתִם is “I made/did them” (H6213, 1cs perfect with 3mp object suffix); עֲשִׂיתֶם is “you (mp) did/made” (2mp perfect). Different person and object-suffix morphology within עשׂה.

## 15. `אהל֣י`

אָהֳלִי is “my tent” (H168, singular with 1cs suffix); אָהֳלֵי / אָהֳלֶי are “tents of” (plural construct). Same אהל noun; number and suffix/construct forms differ.

## Disposition and limitations

All 15 groups represent inflectional variation within the same established lexeme families in the supplied records: construct state, pronominal suffixes, number, or verbal person/conjugation. These orthographic links should not be interpreted as separate semantic lexemes. The source morphology and Strong’s assignments are preserved as-is.
