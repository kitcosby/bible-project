## 1. `כּהנ֥י`

כֹּהֲנֵי is “priests of” (H3548, plural construct); כֹּהֲנַי is “my priests” (plural with 1cs suffix). Same כהן noun.

## 2. `לֽא־ידעתּֽם׃`

לֹא־יְדַעְתֶּם is “you (plural) did not know” (H3045, Qal perfect 2mp); לֹא־יְדַעְתָּם is “you (singular) did not know them” (2ms with 3mp object suffix). Same verb, different person/number and object marking.

## 3. `לֽאלה֣י`

לֵאלֹהֵי is “to/for God(s) of” (H430, plural construct); לֵאלֹהַי is “to/for my God” (same noun with 1cs suffix).

## 4. `לב֣א`

לְבֹא in Numbers 34:8 is parsed as a place-name (HNp); לָבֹא in Genesis 48:7 and Jeremiah 42:15 is the infinitive “to come/enter” (H935). Proper-name versus verbal infinitive; verify the toponym independently before treating the source parse as settled.

## 5. `לך֩`

לְךָ is “to you” (2ms suffix, source Strong’s null); לֵךְ is “go!” (H3212, Qal imperative 2ms). Distinct preposition-plus-suffix and verb, despite identical consonants.

## 6. `מזּבח֖י`

מִזִּבְחִי is “from my altar” (H2077, singular with 1cs suffix); מִזִּבְחֵי is “from altars of” (plural construct).

## 7. `עשׂית֖ם`

עֲשִׂיתִם is “I did/made them” (H6213, 1cs perfect with 3mp object suffix); עֲשִׂיתֶם is “you (plural) did/made” (2mp perfect). Same verb; subject and object morphology differ.

## 8. `פּני֙`

פְּנֵי is “face/surface of” (H6440, construct); פָּנַי is “my face” (same noun with 1cs suffix).

## 9. `צעד֣י`

צְעָדַי is “my steps” (H6806, plural with 1cs suffix); צַעֲדִי is “my step” (singular with 1cs suffix); צַעֲדֵי is “steps of” (plural construct). Same צעד noun, number and construct/suffix distinctions.

## 10. `קדשׁ֣י`

קָדְשִׁי is “my holy one / my holiness” (H6944, singular with 1cs suffix); קָדְשֵׁי is “holy things of” (plural construct). Context is needed to choose the English rendering of the singular.

## 11. `רגל֥י`

רַגְלֵי is “feet of” (H7272, plural construct); רַגְלַי is “my feet” (plural with 1cs suffix).

## 12. `שׁלח֥ה`

שִׁלְחָה is parsed as a 2ms imperative of שלח (“send!”; the source marks a directive/he suffix); שָׁלְחָה is Qal perfect 3fs (“she sent”). Same root, imperative versus finite past/perfect form; exact force of the imperative suffix should be checked in the grammar.

## 13. `שׁמע֤ה`

שִׁמְעָה is a 2ms imperative of שמע (“hear!”; directive/he suffix in source morphology); שָׁמְעָה is Qal perfect 3fs (“she heard”). Same root, imperative versus 3fs perfect.

## 14. `שׂפת֥י`

שְׂפָתַי is “my lips” (H8193, plural with 1cs suffix); שִׂפְתֵי is “lips of” (plural construct).

## 15. `להון֙ (Aramaic)`

לְהוֹן is “to them” (Aramaic 3mp pronominal suffix; Strong’s absent in source); לֶהֱוֺן is an Aramaic verbal form of הוה, “they will be/become” (H1934, imperfect 3mp). These are distinct grammatical units. Both are Aramaic and remain in the Aramaic population.
