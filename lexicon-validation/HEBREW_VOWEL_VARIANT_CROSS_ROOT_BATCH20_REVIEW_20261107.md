## 1. `בּאזנ֖י`

בְּאָזְנֵי is “in the ears of” (H241, plural construct); בְּאָזְנָי is “in my ears” (plural with 1cs suffix). Same אֹזֶן noun; construct versus possessive suffix.

## 2. `בּנ֗י`

בְּנִי is “my son” (H1121, singular with 1cs suffix); בְּנֵי is “sons of” (plural construct). Same בן noun; number and suffix/construct differ.

## 3. `בּעיני֙`

בְּעֵינֵי is “in the eyes of” (H5869, plural construct); בְּעֵינַי is “in my eyes” (plural with 1cs suffix).

## 4. `דּברי֙`

דְּבָרִי is “my word” (H1697, singular with 1cs suffix); דְּבָרַי is “my words” (plural with 1cs suffix); דִּבְרֵי is “words of” (plural construct). Same דבר noun, with singular/plural and construct/suffix distinctions.

## 5. `העל֥ה`

הֶעֱלָה is Hiphil perfect 3ms of עלה (“he brought up/caused to ascend”); הָעֹלָה is an article + Qal participle feminine singular (“the one going up/ascending”). Same verbal root, different stem/form and syntax.

## 6. `וּבחוּר֖י`

וּבַחוּרֵי is “and young men of” (H970, plural construct); וּבַחוּרַי is “and my young men” (plural with 1cs suffix).

## 7. `ואֽמרה֙`

וְאָֽמְרָה is “and she said” (H559, Qal 3fs); וָאֹֽמְרָה is “and I said” (Qal wayyiqtol 1cs). Person differs; context determines subject.

## 8. `ואחר֖י`

וְאַחֲרֵי is “and after” (H310); וְאַחֲרַי is “and after me” (same preposition/adverb with 1cs suffix).

## 9. `ואלה֖י`

וֵאלֹהֵי is “and God(s) of” (H430, plural construct); וֵאלֹהַי is “and my God” (same noun with 1cs suffix). Referent is contextual.

## 10. `ואמר֗ה`

Same underlying contrast as the other אמרה group: וְאָמְרָה “and she said” (3fs) versus וָאֹמְרָה “and I said” (1cs). Separate accent-bearing signature, not a new lexical relationship.

## 11. `ואתּנ֨ה`

וְאֶתְּנָה is a 1cs cohortative form of נתן (“and let me give / and I will give”); וָאֶתְּנָה is 1cs wayyiqtol (“and I gave”). Same verb; mood/aspect and narrative syntax differ.

## 12. `ויד֣י`

וְיָדַי is “and my hands” (H3027, plural with 1cs suffix); וִידֵי is “and hands of” (plural construct).

## 13. `ול֥ך`

וְלֵךְ is “and go!” (H3212, Qal imperative 2ms); וְלָךְ is “and to you” (preposition לְ + 2fs suffix, Strong’s absent in source). Unrelated grammatical/lexical units collide under consonant-only comparison.

## 14. `ועבדתּ֖ם`

וַעֲבַדְתֶּם is parsed as 2mp (“and you plural served/will serve,” context-dependent); וַעֲבַדְתָּם is parsed as 2ms with 3mp object suffix (“and you singular served them/will serve them”). Same עבד verb; person/number and object suffix differ. Rendering requires clause context.

## 15. `ימ֛י`

יְמֵי is “days of” (H3117, plural construct); יָמַי is “my days” (plural with 1cs suffix).

## Notes

Most candidates are inflectional forms of the same lexeme (construct versus suffix, singular versus plural, or verbal person/mood distinctions). The לֵךְ / לָךְ group is a clear unrelated grammatical collision. The two אמרה signatures represent the same person/verb contrast in distinct accent-bearing forms. No root, lexicon, Strong’s, morphology, or alignment data were changed.
