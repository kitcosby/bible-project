## 2. `ימ֥י`

מִפְּנֵי is “from/before the face of”; מִפָּנַי is “from before me” (H6440 with 1cs suffix). Same פנים lexical family, different suffix realization.

## 6. `יד֣י`

## 7. `ידי֙`

## 10. `ל֛ך`

לֵךְ is “go!” (H3212); לָךְ is “to you” (2fs suffix; no Strong’s value in the supplied member record). Preserve the null and do not conflate the meanings.

## 11. `ועשׂית֖ם`

וַעֲשִׂיתֶם is “and you (mp) shall do” (H6213); וַעֲשִׂיתָם is “and you (ms) did them” (same root with a 3mp object suffix). Different person/number and suffix morphology within עשׂה.

## 12. `ל֖ך`

## 14. `אח֖י`

אֲחִי is “brother of”; אַחַי is “my brothers”; אָחִי is “my brother” (H251). Three member keys reflect construct, plural suffix, and singular suffix forms of the same kinship noun.

## 15. `לב֥וא`

לָבוֹא is “to come” (H935, infinitive); לְבוֹא is recorded as a place-name (H935). Verify the proper-name reading independently; the orthographic link must not collapse the place-name and verb.

The לֵךְ / לָךְ groups remain high-risk orthographic collisions; the prepositional member has no Strong’s value in the supplied data, and that absence is preserved. The לְבוֹא place-name assignment requires independent source/geographical verification. All entries remain orthographic candidates only.
